Trusted Deployment Readiness Map

A concise diagnostic for mapping visible progress, institutional evidence, unresolved gaps, and the next controlled commitment.

  1. Technical PromiseThe technology works in principle.
  2. Institutional EvidenceProblem, value, ownership, and decision context are validated.
  3. Controlled CommitmentResources are committed within defined limits.
  4. Trusted DeploymentThe solution is adopted, operated, governed, and accountable.

Readiness Questions

  1. Is the use case specific, important, and understood by the institution?
  2. Is there a clear owner of the problem and an identifiable decision-maker?
  3. Is there a budget, or a credible path to budget?
  4. Is the approval path understood?
  5. Are the main risks identified and allocated in principle?
  6. Is operational responsibility during and after the pilot clear?
  7. Is there a defined basis for progressing from pilot to adoption?
